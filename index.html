Frequently asked questions

What does a freight forwarder do?

A forwarder plans and organises the whole transport: picks the right mode, books capacity, prepares documents and customs, and is your single contact until delivery.

Can I combine road, rail and sea in one booking?

Yes. We plan multimodal routes as one order with one price, for example truck to our rail terminal, train to the port and ship overseas.

How do I track my shipment?

Use "Track shipment" at the top of this page or the customer area. You'll need the tracking number from your delivery note or booking confirmation.
